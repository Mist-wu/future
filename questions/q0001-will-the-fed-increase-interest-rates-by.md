## 题目原文

Will the Fed increase interest rates by 50+ bps after the December 2026 meeting?

## 判定标准（原文）

The FED interest rates are defined in this market by the upper bound of the target federal funds range. The decisions on the target federal funds range are made by the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meetings.

This market will resolve to the amount of basis points the upper bound of the target federal funds rate is changed by versus the level it was prior to the Federal Reserve's December 2026 meeting.

If the target federal funds rate is changed to a level not expressed in the displayed options, the change will be rounded up to the nearest 25 and will resolve to the relevant bracket. (e.g. if there's a cut/increase of 12.5 bps it will be considered to be 25 bps)

The resolution source for this market is the FOMC’s statement after its meeting scheduled for December 8-9, 2026 according to the official calendar: https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm.

The level and change of the target federal funds rate is also published at the official website of the Federal Reserve at https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/openmarket.htm.

This market may resolve as soon as the FOMC’s statement for their December meeting with relevant data is issued. If no statement is released by the end date of the next scheduled meeting, this market will resolve to the "No change" bracket.

## 判定标准（中文）

利率指联邦基金目标区间的上限。按 2026 年 12 月 8 日至 9 日 FOMC 会议声明公布的上限相对会前的变动结算。变动不是 25 个基点的整数倍时，向上取整到最近的 25 个基点。到会议结束日还没有声明，按“不变”结算。本题问的是“加息 50 个基点或更多”这一档。

## 预测记录

### 2026-11-13 首次预测（单次，AI 盲测）

已见市场价：11 月 11 日查新闻时见到 Polymarket 上这一档约 12.5%，CME FedWatch 约 14% 到 15%。这个盲测值受过它们的影响。

1. **基准率**。1994 年以来美联储大约加息 50 次，其中 50 个基点或更多的约 11 次，占两成左右，集中在 1994 年和 2022 年这两段明显落后于通胀的时期。另一个参照：会前四周利率期货给大幅动作定价 15% 左右时，美联储通常会在静默期前把预期引导到位，很少意外。
2. **证据**。支持：理事 Waller 会前说，核心 CPI 环比达到 0.3% 就应该考虑加 50 个基点，只加 25 会伤害信誉，而 10 月核心 CPI 正是 0.3%；达拉斯联储的 Logan 表示可能要加快；芝加哥联储的 Goolsbee 称数据“接近令人不安”。反对：费城联储的 Paulson 支持“渐进”收紧；高盛认为不会直接加 50；期货市场给 25 个基点的概率约 88%。来源：[Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/fed-s-waller-backs-december-rate-hike-says-open-to-larger-move)、[TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/fed/fed-waller-jumbo-interest-rate-hike-december-inflation)、[Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/fed-rate-hike-odds-in-december-at-88-after-hot-cpi-cme-fedwatch-5017585)。
3. **调整**。从期货隐含的 14% 出发。公开支持 50 的只有 Waller 一人，主席 Warsh 还没有表态，会前只剩 12 月 4 日的非农这一个重磅数据，下调到 12%。
4. **反向设想**。如果最后加了 50：最可能是 Warsh 在静默期前公开为 50 铺路，或者非农和 PCE 再次大超预期。这条路径不难想象，所以没有压到 10% 以下。
5. **概率和触发条件**。概率 12%。会让我明显改判断的消息：Warsh 或两位以上理事公开支持 50；FedWatch 上 50 的概率升过 35% 或跌破 5%；12 月 4 日非农数据。

### 2026-11-14 复查，无变化

没有触发重估条件。主席 Warsh 没有新的公开表态。10 月 PPI 略低于预期，但经济学家认为不足以排除 50 个基点。CME FedWatch 上 50 个基点的概率在 11% 到 12%，没有越过 35% 或 5% 的线。来源：[InvestmentNews](https://investmentnews.com/equities/ppi-still-uncomfortably-high-as-december-rate-hike-looms/269002)。
